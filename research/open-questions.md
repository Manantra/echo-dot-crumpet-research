Determine if this comes from formatting, per-device differences, or other content before claiming identity.

8. **Partly resolved:** the updated guard's encoded BSS interval `[0x00102180,0x00109DAC)` contains the published payload's BDEV target `0x001086EC`; its `0x00108804` data extent would overlap protected BSS if the effective destination is zero. [Static source/bin check](../docs/sram-guard-exploit-intersection.md). **Partly resolved:** the TEE-header parser at `0x20F368`, state-driven address query `0x2160F4`, conditional calculator `0x216130`, guard at `0x20F490`, and subsequent bulk read are now corroborated by decoded Thumb calls in both 2023-era builds ([details](../docs/tee-header-address-processing.md)). **Still open:** determine the exact effective address for `0xFFFFFFFF` under all states and whether alternate parsing paths can skip the range checks; do not claim full mitigation without this proof.

9. **Partly resolved:** we verified LK binaries from official 2021–2025 OTAs and decoded the Fastboot `flash:` dispatcher, `unlock` validation at FILE+0x1F804 and separate `otucert`/`otucode` handlers at FILE+0x1F85E / +0x1F87C ([LK timeline](../docs/lk-image-timeline.md), [call graph](../docs/lk-fastboot-unlock-disassembly.md)). **Still open:** identify any independently documented legitimate Amazon signing-certificate issuance/authorization; do not infer that accepting command names permits unsigned unlock.
10. **Partly resolved:** source tracing shows the DA2 failure is an **exception or incomplete protocol reply** after transfer acknowledgement, not a diagnosed DRAM/NAND/security error. The two [distinct MT8167 DA2 binaries](../docs/da2-binary-emi-compatibility.md) have different drivers and BSS regions, but both fit within the **256 MiB `[0x40000000,0x50000000)`** measured in independent public Crumpet normal-boot logs. Four official preloader EMI trailers are byte-identical. [Our protocol/memory-map audit](../docs/da2-handoff-status-memory.md) identifies a 12-byte XFLASH status header and distinguishes returned error from missing USB response. **Still open:** determine whether Stage 2 executes any instructions on failing Crumpet hardware, classify its precise transport exception, and identify the selected DA filename/hash in existing logs. No blind hardware tests.
